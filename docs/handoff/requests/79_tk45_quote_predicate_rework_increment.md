# TK-45 quotation and finite-predicate rework

This increment supersedes the blanket balanced-quotation masking described in receipt 77. A quoted term with an external case particle or an emphasized author conclusion remains available as evidence. Only an outer speech relation confirmed by existing report/citation polarity excludes the quoted payload. Unresolved quotative relations and unclosed delimiters preserve an explicit verification request. Projection and quotation roles are computed once in the original statement, then sliced without reparsing partial quoted spans.

Configured requirement arguments now bind generic lexical predicates with attached negative auxiliaries. A negative auxiliary must itself end the governing clause; an embedded reported/adnominal predicate or nonfinite negative connective remains uncertain. Independent argument changes, contextual adjuncts, nested negation and configured absent-fact polarities keep their previous handling. No new domain terms, sample verb list, rule ID or configuration entry is introduced. Requirement, governing-predicate, negative-morpheme, quotation-role and conclusion diagnostics use local statement coordinates; they do not claim document-global provenance.

Related verification covers 203 checks: the unchanged 175-check suite plus 28 new actual TXT pipeline controls. New pairs cover three author emphasis forms, four lexical predicate denial/fulfilment pairs, two cited and two opponent quoted propositions, two rebuttals with denied/fulfilled opposites, unresolved quotative relations, nonfinite negatives and embedded predicate reports. Existing R6-02/R7-03/R7-08, TK40, normal defenses, absent-fact polarity and general polarity controls retain their expected values.

The unquoted opponent-requirement attribution limitation exists at the prior base and remains unresolved. This is bounded grammatical scope analysis, not a complete Korean semantic parser. Root owns fresh independent public audit, private normal/denial/extension measurements and exact-candidate CI; no private performance or full ticket acceptance is claimed. Protected files, expectations, score baselines, version and deployment settings are unchanged. No full `verify_all` was run.

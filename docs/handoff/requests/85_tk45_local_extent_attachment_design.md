# TK-45 supplement: local extent attachment across a predicate chain

**Design only; product change awaits evaluator approval.** Public actual-TXT audit of `0078572` found that the meaning layer scans extent only after the first incidental negative connective. It therefore incorrectly supports a whole requirement completion when a partitive appears immediately before the requirement object or earlier inside that same chain. A bounded completion sense cannot resolve this extent ambiguity.

**Proposed contract.** Gather the existing source-grounded partitive/degree evidence over the requirement's full local predicate span, including the portion before its incidental negative connective. Also retain an immediately contiguous partitive modifier before the matched requirement argument, bounded by the preceding clause/punctuation boundary. Prefix attachment requires only whitespace or other recognized extent modifiers between the extent and the argument; intervening subjects, objects, actions and arbitrary words do not inherit extent. Keep each original local source span and an attachment label (argument-prefix or predicate-chain); do not scan the document or borrow another independent clause's qualifiers. A qualifier with unresolved attachment inside the same chain blocks newly asserted whole-fulfillment support and keeps a verification request; it does not establish DENIED or resolve whether the qualifier applies to delay or fulfillment.

**Controls frozen before product changes.** New generated actual-TXT and direct-observation pairs cover extent before the argument, before the incidental negative, and after it. Opposites cover an earlier independently completed other-object act, a qualifier on another subject, unqualified fulfillment and the unrelated lexeme `일부러`. Evidence must retain the source-aligned qualifier and set both asserted-fulfillment support and total-completion assertion false. Existing source-sense/classification records are reused; no lemma exception, public-sample synonym list or new meaning resource is needed. Existing210 expectations and the original normal/denial/extension conditions remain unchanged.

**Bounds and validation.** Prefix plus local-chain scanning stays linear in the existing bounded review unit; no global inheritance or new rule ID. Preserve quotation projection, finite assertion/argument/conflict checks and legacy single-positive provenance. Estimated implementation and related checks: approximately 20–30 minutes, then exact final-SHA CI and root's independent gates. `0078572` review request is held; no acceptance claim follows from this correction.

Pre-implementation public contract result on unchanged `0078572` product: **4 failed, 7 passed**. The failures are the two argument-prefix and two pre-connective extent controls; existing post-connective extent and all six opposite controls pass. The failures are intentionally exposed rather than weakened or marked as accepted behavior.

# TK-45 supplement: grounded predicate meaning and asserted fulfillment

**Design only; new semantic classification awaits evaluator approval.** The approved `a307d6b` increment preserves an unresolved predicate chain instead of hard DENIED, but that alone cannot restore the mandatory no-new-warning normal gate. Existing configured requirement expressions and explicit finite assertions remain evidence; a same-object positive verb alone is insufficient. No private inputs or scores inform this design.

**Public basis, retrieved 2026-10-11.** The National Institute of Korean Language's Korean Basic Dictionary distinguishes [완료하다](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/m/kor/searchResult?mainSearchWord=완료하다), “완전히 끝마치다”; [이행하다](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/m/kor/searchResult?mainSearchWord=이행하다), 履行 “약속이나 계약 등을 실제로 행하다” versus 移行 changing state; [수행하다](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/m/kor/searchResult?mainSearchWord=수행하다), 遂行 doing work as conceived/planned versus 修行/隨行; [실행하다](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/m/kor/searchResult?mainSearchWord=실행하다), actual performance versus running a computer program; and [중단하다](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/m/kor/searchResult?mainSearchWord=중단하다), stopping an undertaking midway. These definitions establish general COMPLETION, REALIZATION, INTERRUPTION and unresolved-sense categories. They do not establish legal sufficiency or exempt any negative word. The same dictionary supplies further completion, interruption/deferment and prospective-plan entries; only individually retrieved, sense-grounded entries may enter the resource. A completion auxiliary also completes its own event, never an unrelated requirement: [국립국어원 -어 버리다](https://m.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=216&qna_seq=322741&pageIndex=1&searchCondition=&searchKeyword=) explains that the preceding action has ended.

**Contract.** A small versioned lexical-semantic resource records lemma, sense, category and public source, independent of legal nouns and sample sentences. Recognition uses grammatical conjugation, exact token/lemma boundaries and argument attachment, not warning-exemption prefix lists or synonym expansion. A COMPLETION/REALIZATION sense may support POSITIVE only when its own unnegated past finite matrix assertion directly governs the configured requirement's event object, the same author and case-bound argument remain, and no independent object, nominal/adnominal embedding, plan/preparation head, modal/prospective scope, report or conflicting denial intervenes. The source spans, sense and attachment are retained as evidence. A completed plan, paperwork, interruption, postponement or another act cannot certify the requirement. Homonymous senses without discriminating argument evidence remain verification-needed. A negative completion predicate remains DENIED; nested/conflicting polarity remains uncertain. A final assertion does not erase an independently denied requirement or authenticate the underlying facts.

**Opposing controls and limits.** Before implementation, actual TXT pairs must cover at least three independently worded completion/performance assertions against interruption/deferment/abandonment and unresolved predicates, plus negative completion, bare nouns, future plans, completed preparations/plans/other objects, quotation, opponent reports and authored rebuttals (speaker categories two-plus). Exercise the meaning classifier on ordinary nonlegal event objects too, so legal vocabulary cannot choose the category. Preserve the existing210 and approved chain/consequence controls without editing expectations; the new resource's own completion cases replace no frozen acceptance case. Unknown meanings stay explicit requests. No new rule ID, private corpus, model request/schema/prompt/cache change, protected edit or baseline/version/deployment change. Proposed semantic work: approximately one additional hour after approval, followed by root's original normal-preservation, denial/extension and exact-SHA gates; public semantic success cannot replace those gates.

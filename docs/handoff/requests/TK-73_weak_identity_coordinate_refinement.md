# TK-73 weak-identity / coordinate refinement

Public audit of `be654f9` found two genuine-duplicate losses despite verified provenance/fields: multiple distinct weak captions collapse under one generic narrative anchor; a coordinate unit can prefix-match a longer Korean word. This refinement retains the approved provenance model and conservative identity contract. It uses no private raw inputs or lexical exception list.

**Conflicting identities:** collect the normalized complete fallback identity of every weak locator under each full proposed title in its numbering group. One independent nonlocator narrative can corroborate a title boundary only when that title has one weak-caption identity. Multiple distinct weak alternatives remain whole; their generic common title cannot erase their disagreement. Set collection remains linear and respects party/number/branch scope. Repeated identical weak captions still corroborate.

**Complete coordinate tokens:** require the numeric-unit right edge to meet positive observed whitespace, delimiter punctuation or text end. Unicode separator categories / actual ASCII whitespace and delimiter punctuation categories are accepted; format, nonwhitespace control, symbol and connector-punctuation categories never certify a boundary. The complement of letter/number/mark classes is not proof. Attached Korean letters without independent observed token-boundary proof cannot certify a coordinate; this treats longer lexical words uniformly rather than naming exceptions. No-space prefixes before digits are still supported. Physical item/continuation/block edges establish origin positions, not word boundaries: `제7행` followed by a new line `성 탐사` must stay ambiguous. Fragment-only boundaries cannot certify locator identity. An opaque unit-plus-letter suffix, whether single-source or reflowed, remains ambiguous and retains full comparison, including when a generic narrative is present. Consequently the previous public positive with an opaque attached qualifier becomes a documented residual, not evidence that the whole suffix is a locator; its no-space-prefix coverage will use a separated qualifier, and the opaque form will gain explicit ambiguity controls.

**Checks:** add actual generated TXT/PDF controls with/without sections for same-group distinct weak captions plus an anchor; longer words beginning with page/line unit sequences; format/control/symbol/connector characters versus observed separator/punctuation boundaries; and repeated complete-token locator identities. Retain supplied unpunctuated reflow as an explicit UNKNOWN/full-comparison false-positive residual; positive reflow requires an observed delimiter or space at the unit edge. Preserve original fields/source projections, all-date/protected title cases, genuine A grade, TK-64 and 0.1-second new-work benchmark. TXT must preserve uncertain source characters; the public PDF font omits some format/control glyphs, in which case tests inspect the actual extracted lexical word rather than claim glyph preservation. Existing protected/original tests remain unchanged. This is a bounded guard on the approved weak proof, not blanket suppression or a new finding path. Independent formal acceptance and the fresh seal remain evaluator-owned.
